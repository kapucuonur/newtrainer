The service

ROADLAB lets you ride routes on a map with a Bluetooth smart trainer and heart-rate sensor. The service is free. Features may change, and we may stop the service. If we shut down accounts, we will tell you in advance where we can, so you can export your ride summaries.

Safety first

Indoor cycling is physical exercise. Ride within your limits, set up your bike and trainer as the manufacturer instructs, and keep the area around you clear. ROADLAB is a training tool, not a medical device: power, heart rate, speed and elevation values are estimates and can be wrong. If you feel pain, dizziness, chest discomfort or are unwell, stop riding and see a professional. Check with a doctor before starting training if you have a health condition.
