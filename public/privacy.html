ROADLAB Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026 · Terms of Use

ROADLAB is an indoor cycling app: you connect a smart trainer and heart-rate strap over Bluetooth and ride routes you pick on a world map. This policy explains what data the app uses, where it goes and how to delete it.

Who is responsible

ROADLAB is developed and operated by Onur Kapucu ("we"), who is the controller of your personal data under the GDPR. Contact: onur@trihonor.com.

Without an account

You can ride without an account. Then nothing about you is stored on our server. Your language and map style choices stay on your device. Ride files (FIT/GPX) are created on your device and only saved where you choose.

With an account (optional)

If you create an account, we store on our own server (located in the EU):

  • Email address and password: the password is stored only as a secure hash.
  • Profile you enter: display name, body weight, bike weight, FTP.
  • Ride summaries you choose to save: date, duration, distance, average/maximum power, heart rate, cadence, speed, elevation gain and route name. GPS tracks and ride files are not stored.
  • Group rides: while you are in a group ride, your display name and position on the shared route are shown to the other riders in that room.

We use this data only to run your account and these features. We do not sell it, show ads or use analytics or tracking tools.

Bluetooth

The app uses Bluetooth only to connect to your trainer and heart-rate sensor. Power, cadence, speed and heart rate are read live during the ride. The app does not use Bluetooth to determine your location.

Map and route services

To show maps and build routes, the app sends the map area, the points you pick and your search text to these services. Like any website, they also see your IP address:

ServicePurpose
OpenFreeMap, Esri (ArcGIS)Map and satellite tiles
OSRM (project-osrm.org)Route building
OpenStreetMap NominatimPlace search
Open-Meteo, OpenTopoDataElevation along the route
Google Street View, MapillaryStreet-level photos during the ride
YouTubeRide videos, only if you open the video panel
Google FontsFonts

The app does not read your device's location. Street View imagery is provided by Google; Google's use of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Legal basis and retention

We process account data to provide the service you sign up for (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)). We keep it until you delete your account. Our server's request logs do not contain your IP address and are rotated regularly.

Hosting

The website is hosted by Vercel, and traffic to our server passes through Cloudflare. Both process your IP address and request data only to deliver and protect the service.

Security

All connections use HTTPS. Access to your account data requires your login.

Delete your account and data

In the app: open Account, tap Delete account, enter your password and confirm. Your account, profile, saved ride summaries and group rooms you host are deleted immediately and permanently.

Without the app: log in at newtrainer.trihonor.com and do the same under Account, or email onur@trihonor.com from the address of your account and we will delete it within 30 days.

Your rights

You can see and change your profile in the app at any time and download your ride summaries as FIT files. Under the GDPR you may ask for access, correction, export or deletion of your data, and you can complain to your data protection authority.

Children

ROADLAB is not directed at children under 16.

Health

ROADLAB is a training tool, not a medical device. If you feel pain, dizziness or are unwell, stop riding and see a professional.
